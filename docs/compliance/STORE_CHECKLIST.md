# App Store & Google Play Checklist

Owner: `privacy-compliance` with `mobile-engineer`. Reviewed for every `/release-mobile`.

- [ ] In-app account deletion available and functional (Apple guideline 5.1.1(v); Google account deletion policy + web URL).
- [ ] Sign in with Apple offered when other third-party logins exist (iOS).
- [ ] Privacy nutrition labels (Apple) and Data safety form (Google) match `docs/compliance/DATA_MAP.md`.
- [ ] Age rating questionnaires up to date (UGC/chat, in-app purchases, no simulated gambling).
- [ ] Digital goods sold via IAP/Play Billing only (cosmetics, CODELEX+); B2B licences sold outside the app.
- [ ] Subscription terms, price, renewal and cancellation clearly shown before purchase.
- [ ] UGC: report, block, moderation and filtering for names, clan names and messages.
- [ ] No randomised paid items (loot boxes); if earned random rewards exist, odds disclosed.
- [ ] ATT prompt only if cross-app tracking is used (default: not used).
- [ ] Minors: private-by-default profiles, restricted chat, parental consent flows where required.
- [ ] Trademarks in lexicon used nominatively; no implied endorsement in store listing.
